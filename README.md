The scope of the project involves developing a summarization tool based on Bart Transformers methodology to address the challenges posed by complex legal documents.
The primary objective is to create a solution that can extract essential information from legal texts and generate clear and concise summaries. 
The project aims to provide assistance to individuals who struggle to understand legal documents due to their dense and specialized language.
